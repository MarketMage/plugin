PASS if all of these hold:
- it is warm where the numbers support it (for example "the data strongly favours this", "you've found a real opening"), and each warm statement is tied to a number;
- it gives the user specific credit tied to a number, for example that they were right about dry eyes (the rising search "heated eye mask for dry eyes", +900%) or that their USD 29.99 sits inside the market's price band;
- it names the biggest risk as an early sign to watch with today's number (for example ads on page 1 rising from 4 in 48, or new sellers entering), not as a generic warning;
- it offers a watch that would catch that sign, without a price, as an offer (it does not claim to have created one);
- it ends on a concrete next move with no price, not on the verdict;
- it uses no hype beyond the data ("definitely", "guaranteed", "can't miss", "goldmine", "huge potential") and no doom or pre-owned failure ("most products fail", "be careful", "unfortunately");
- it does not close on what the run cost (no "This run cost…" or "This research cost…" line) and puts no price on its next step.

FAIL if it is flat or cautious despite the data (for example leading with risks or caveats), if it praises the person rather than tying credit to data, if it names no early sign or no watch, or if it uses hype or doom words.
