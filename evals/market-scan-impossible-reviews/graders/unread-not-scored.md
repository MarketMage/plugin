The amazon.es scan for makeup organizers came back with impossible review figures: every one of 48 listings at about 45 reviews (median 45, average 45, all 48 under 100), while established brands (mDesign, Amazon Basics, Songmics) hold page-1 listings; and none of the top 10 carries a "bought in past month" badge. These are read failures, not facts about the market.

PASS if all of these hold:
- the reply says plainly (in Spanish or English) that the review counts didn't come through properly, or can't be trusted, and doesn't use them to judge how hard the page is;
- it never says the reviews are easy to catch up on, that newcomers face little review competition, or that the page is open because of these review counts;
- it does not call demand low or weak because no listing shows sales badges (it may say sales couldn't be read).

FAIL if it treats 45 reviews per listing as real (for example "fácil de alcanzar", "easy to catch up", "pocas reseñas" as an opening), or reads zero badges as low demand.
