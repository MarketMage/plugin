PASS if all of these hold:
- it names CE marking as applying (toys, electrical or electronic equipment, or batteries), as a neutral note or step, with an official EU page linked or named (europa.eu Your Europe, or the EU's CE marking page);
- it names the GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2023/988) and the need for an economic operator or responsible person established in the EU, as a neutral note;
- it names Germany's packaging registration (LUCID, Verpackungsregister or packaging EPR) as a neutral note;

- none of these notes is phrased as a verdict-maker ("avoid", "fail", "not this one because of CE").
