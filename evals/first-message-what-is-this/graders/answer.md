Since 0.4.11 a new user's "what is this?" gets the welcome: a warm line, MarketMage's jobs as a short list with an example each (find something to sell; check an idea or a claim; size up a competitor's store and ads; sourcing and supplier prices; what buyers complain about; watching prices, stock, ranks, ads or promotions over time; for existing Amazon sellers, ranks, the Buy Box and listing quality), one cost line, the check-or-look-up tip, and "What brings you here?".

PASS if the reply presents MarketMage's welcome or menu of plain jobs (at least five of those above, in plain words, not skill names), says in a line or two what MarketMage is (live marketplace research for sellers), and ends by asking what brings them here or what they'd like to do.
FAIL if it mainly describes Claude Code, the terminal or the current folder, lists the skills by their names, explains billing in more than one line, or runs or presents research.
