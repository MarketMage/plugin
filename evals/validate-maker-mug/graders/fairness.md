PASS if the reply contains a plain line on fairness to other makers that says, in its own words, both: (1) reading shops' public listings or prices (what a shopper browsing sees) and pricing her own work at a price that sustains it is fair; and (2) copying another maker's design (or photos or listing words) is not. One or two sentences, without a lecture.

FAIL if there is no fairness line, or it only covers one of the two points, or it tells her she shouldn't look at other shops.
