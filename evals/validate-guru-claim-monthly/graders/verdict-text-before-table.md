PASS if all of these hold:
- after the plain answer to the claim and before the signal table, the reply gives a written verdict: a market label ("Strong", "Promising, with one fix" or "Not this version") in a sentence of text, with why in a number or two;
- the verdict is not carried only by a chart or diagram title, and no chart or table appears before that written verdict;
- the market label agrees with the counts the reply itself states, by this rule: Strong needs the margin not against and either 5 or more for with at most 1 against, or 4 or more for with none against; Not this version is 4 or more against, or margin and concentration both against, or 2 or more against with more against than for; anything else is Promising, with one fix. (If no counts are stated, judge the label against the signals it lists as helping and hurting.)

FAIL if there is no written verdict before the table, if the only verdict is a chart title, or if the label contradicts the reply's own counts (for example "Promising" with 1 for and 2 against).
