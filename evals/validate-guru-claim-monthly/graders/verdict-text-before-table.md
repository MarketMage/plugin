PASS if all of these hold:
- after the plain answer to the claim and before the signal table, the reply gives a written verdict: a market label ("Strong", "Promising, with one fix" or "Not this version") in a sentence of text, with why in a number or two;
- the verdict is not carried only by a chart or diagram title, and no chart or table appears before that written verdict;
- the market label agrees with the counts the reply itself states, by this rule: Strong needs the margin not against and either 5 or more for with at most 1 against, or 4 or more for with none against; Not this version is 4 or more against, or margin and concentration both against, or 2 or more against with more against than for plus unknown (more against than the for and the unread signals together); when against outnumber for only until the unread signals are counted, the label is Promising, with one fix; anything else is Promising, with one fix. (If no counts are stated, judge the label against the signals it lists as helping and hurting.)
- whenever the reply's own counts have more against than for and the label is "Promising, with one fix", the verdict sentence itself says in plain words why the against signals don't rule it out (what is still unread and could turn it, or what outweighs them), before anything hopeful (real-user test round 2, R2-13).

FAIL if there is no written verdict before the table, if the only verdict is a chart title, if the label contradicts the reply's own counts (for example "Promising" with 1 for and 2 against and nothing unread), or if "Promising" stands beside more against than for with no line saying why that doesn't rule it out.
