The user makes her candles and sells them only on her own Shopify store (not on Etsy), at USD 32, with a making cost of USD 4.20 materials plus 40 minutes at USD 18 an hour (USD 16.20 in all). The plugin can't read her own store, so it reads Etsy.

PASS if all of these hold:
- the reply says, in its own words, that Etsy was read as a reference (handmade reference, comparison or benchmark) for her, and does not present Etsy as the marketplace she sells on or tell her she pays Etsy's fees;
- the margin it gives as hers is after her store's payment or card fee (her figure, or an assumed card fee such as 2.9% + USD 0.30, said as an assumption), not after Etsy's fees (9.5% + USD 0.45 or similar), and uses her own making cost (materials and her time; about USD 14 to 15 left a candle at USD 32, or the materials-only figure labelled as before her time);
- no supplier, factory or Made-in-China cost is used as her cost, and no first-order or MOQ gate is applied.

FAIL if Etsy is treated as her own sales channel (its fees taken from her price as hers, or "your Etsy listing"), if the margin is worked out on a supplier cost, or if the reply gives no margin for her when she gave her cost.
