R3-19, one peak. The Trends data read in this check rises every December (the highest month of each year, 2021 to 2025) and has a single one-off high in April 2026. The yearly peak is December; April 2026 is a one-off high, not "the peak".

PASS if every mention of when searches peak names the same month, and that month is December (for example "peaks every December", "the December peak is under 90 days away"); or if the reply names no peak month at all. A separate mention of April 2026 is fine only when it is called a one-off high (or similar) and not "the peak" or "peaked".

FAIL if two different months are each called the peak (for example a line saying searches "peaked April 2026" beside one saying they peak in December), or if April 2026 is given as the peak that a timing or lead-time line relies on.
