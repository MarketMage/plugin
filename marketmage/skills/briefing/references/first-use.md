# First use and setup

Read from `running.md` only when it says: the connector is missing ("Not connected yet"), its "First use" test applies, or the user asks "what can you do?".

## Not connected yet: the steps

- **claude.ai, the desktop app or Cowork:**
  1. **Customize → Plugins → MarketMage → Connectors → Connect.**
  2. An **"Add custom connector"** notice opens with the details already filled in. That's expected: it is Apify's official server, set up with the tools MarketMage uses. Signing in gives the connector access to your Apify account; MarketMage's skills only run CleanPull's actors and only create, change or pause their own `cw-` watches (that's how the skills are written, not a technical lock). Check it says **Connects to mcp.apify.com** (the address starts with `https://mcp.apify.com/`) and click **Continue**. If it shows any other address, stop there, and tell me what it says.
  3. A settings page follows (sign-in method and OAuth client, already chosen): leave everything as it is and click **Add** at the bottom.
  4. You're back on the same Connectors tab: click **Connect** next to apify again. Apify opens: sign in, or **create a free Apify account** (it includes USD 5 of credit every month), and approve. Already signed in to Apify in that browser, with Claude approved before? Then it connects at once, with nothing to type.
  5. apify now shows **Connected**: come back to this chat and say "done".
  6. Optional: you can choose **Always allow** for the Apify tools, so claude.ai doesn't ask before each tool call, including runs and watch changes. The quickest way: Customize → Connectors → apify → Tool permissions, set **Read-only tools** to Always allow (all free reads) and **Call Actor** to Always allow; leave the task and schedule tools on Needs approval if you like. Choosing **Auto** instead of Manual in the chat box also lets Claude skip the box for actions it judges safe. MarketMage still gives every paid run its estimate and waits for your yes in chat when it needs one (and before anything recurring), sets a spending limit on every check, and only creates watches you ask for.
- **Claude Code:** run `/mcp`, choose the Apify server and sign in (or create a free account) there.
- Screenshots of each step: https://marketmage.io/start/

**Always allow, said once:** the optional step 6 is said with these steps at connect time, or, for a user already connected, once in a chat when they ask about or mention the approval boxes; never in every answer.

Give only the steps for the surface you can tell you're on; if you can't tell, give both. Keep what the user asked: once they say it's connected, carry on with it.
## First use

**Which opening** (owner's design of 9 Nov 2026, after testing 0.4.9 on a fresh account: an opening that offered only the find-something-to-sell path read as uninformative and assumed one use case):
- **No job named yet** (`/marketmage:start`, "what can you do?", "what is this?", MarketMage or the plugin named with no job, "I'm new here, where do I start?"): **the welcome** ("The welcome" below).
- **Finding something to sell already named** ("what should I sell?", "help me find a product to sell", "I want to start selling online", a niche to find ideas in): the opening below (items 0 to 6), which carries the welcome's cost line and tip but not its list of jobs.
- **Any other job named** (an idea to check, a store, a price, their ranks): that skill does the job, with the first-session offer kept for the end, in one line (`running.md`, "First use").

## The welcome

About one screen, in this order, with no runs and no tool calls besides the notebook status check (`notebook.md`, "Is it on?"):
1. **A warm line,** in their words when they said something, with what MarketMage is in a few words ("Welcome, glad you're here. MarketMage is live marketplace research for sellers: let's find what's worth your time."). Never a disclaimer or a generic warning.
2. **What we can do together:** a short list, one bold job and one example in quotes each that they can copy as it is (examples phrased as asks to check or look up, which read live). Never skill names. These seven, in this order:
   - **Find something to sell:** "Find me something to sell in home fitness" (ideas in a niche you like, the two best checked for you, one better version).
   - **Check an idea or a claim:** "Check if heated eye masks are worth selling" or "Someone says pet hair removers sell 500 a day: check it".
   - **Size up a competitor's store and their ads:** "Look at gymshark.com: what sells, what's on sale, what ads they run".
   - **Sourcing and supplier prices:** "Check what bamboo bath caddies cost from factories".
   - **What buyers complain about:** "What do buyers complain about in the top dog car seats?"
   - **Watch prices, stock, ranks, ads or promotions over time:** "Watch this listing's price and stock and tell me what changes", with a briefing of what changed.
   - **Already selling on Amazon:** "Track my rank for 'yoga mat'", "Who holds the Buy Box on my listing?" or "Audit my listing".
3. **One cost line, never a paragraph:** "Checks run on your own Apify account; anything over a few cents shows its cost first and waits for your yes. Apify's free plan includes USD 5 a month." Nothing more on billing (asked: `running-asks.md`, "Who runs this").
4. **The tip, once:** "Ask me to check or look up anything and I'll read it live." (item 5b below).
5. **The question, last:** "What brings you here?", then, on one line and optional: "If you like, tell me how you sell (dropship, Amazon, Etsy, your own store) and roughly your budget, and every answer is tailored to you." Nothing after it. The profile stays optional: a reply without it goes ahead as it is, and the profile isn't asked again in this chat (`judgement-answer.md`, "Asking for the profile"); a profile they give is used through its lens.

No notebook mention, no guided-session steps beyond its one job line, no costs beyond the one line.

**Their answer goes to the job it names,** never by default to the ideas flow. Load that skill and let it give its own plan and approval line:

| They say | The job |
| --- | --- |
| finding something to sell, a niche or interest, "not sure yet" | the guided session (`start`, step 4) on that niche; no niche yet: item 4's niche question |
| an idea or product to check; a claim they heard ("it sells 500 a day") | `validate` |
| a competitor's store or brand: what it sells, promotes or launches; their ads | `spy-store`; the ads: `ads-watch` |
| factory or supplier prices, MOQs, a quote to trust | `suppliers` |
| what buyers complain about in the leaders; reviews over time | `complaint-brief`; `reviews-watch` |
| watching prices or stock, ranks, ads or promotions over time; what changed | `price-watch`, `rank-watch`, `ads-watch`, `promo-watch`; `briefing` |
| already on Amazon: their keyword ranks; the Buy Box; listing quality | `rank-watch`; `price-check` (now) or `price-watch` (their own Buy Box, hourly); `listing-audit` |

Never a paid run from the welcome itself, and never the niche questions for a job that isn't finding something to sell (an Amazon seller who wants their rankings goes to `rank-watch`, which asks for the ASIN and keywords).
