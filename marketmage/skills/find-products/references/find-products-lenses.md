# Find products: the lens steps

The parts of the `find-products` skill read only with a profile (or a fulfilment or channel the user states, such as dropshipping with ads), once the lens is chosen, in the same batch as the lens's file. Everything else is in `SKILL.md`.

**Search demand reads as a caution for cold traffic, and thin stays thin.** Through the cold-traffic lens, TikTok units count once (felt want); an idea whose demand is all Amazon search (a high chart position, high Trends interest, little on TikTok) is not a lead for them: its buyers search Amazon. Low Amazon demand never makes an idea a lead either: with thin TikTok units too, demand is thin for everyone. Say so in its row, with the numbers.

**The single-unit check's cap,** in every lens that runs it: its share of the request's spending limit (max(1.5 × the estimate, the estimate + USD 0.15), never said, `references/running.md`, item 6), never a cap near its estimate, so every idea's product pages are read (a cap near the estimate once stopped a read after the first idea). It is `cleanpull/aliexpress-scraper` with the input in `references/running-runs.md`, "AliExpress prices", `shipTo` the buyer's country. When the user asked for source prices, it covers every idea the answer shows, not only the top 3 (this skill's step 2).

## Step 2, cold-traffic offers

- **Where you'd compete (cold-traffic dropship profiles):** offer, with the shortlist, the saturation read on the top 3 for them: `cleanpull/ad-library-monitor` with `keywords`: the 3 ideas, `platforms: ["meta"]` (add `"tiktok"` only for a market in the EU, the UK or Switzerland: its library covers only those), `countries: ["<market country>"]`, `activeOnly: true`, `oneRowPerAdvertiser: true`, `maxAdsPerTarget: 30`. Cost per idea: 1 watch item USD 0.001 + 1 library page USD 0.0035 + up to 30 advertiser rows × USD 0.0015 ≈ USD 0.05 at most; 3 ≈ **USD 0.15 at most** (USD 0.18 on Apify's free plan from 2026-11-07, when `library-request` becomes USD 0.007 there). With `"tiktok"` added (the EU, the UK, Switzerland): about USD 0.06 more an idea (1 watch item + 3 library pages of 12 ads + up to 30 more rows), so about USD 0.11 an idea and 3 ≈ **USD 0.33 at most** (USD 0.38 on the free plan from 2026-11-07). With a yes. Read it as `references/judgement-saturation.md`, "Where you'd compete" sets out (active, proven and new advertisers; the top 3 TikTok shops' share from the idea's rows in the TikTok read), and put its reading in the idea's For you note ("a fit, with one fix: 14 Meta advertisers, crowded"). In the guided first session `validate` reads it instead.
  - **Outside the US it is part of the check, not an offer.** TikTok Shop can't be read there, so this read is Felt want's stand-in (`references/judgement-lens-cold-traffic-count.md`, "Felt want outside the US"). For a cold-traffic dropship seller outside the US (a profile, or a request that says they'll dropship with TikTok or Meta ads), put it in the first estimate with the standard set: about USD 0.09 for the chart and Trends + the ads read on the top 3 (above: Meta and TikTok in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, about USD 0.33 at most; Meta only elsewhere, about USD 0.15 at most), so about **USD 0.42** in the EU, the UK and Switzerland (USD 0.47 on Apify's free plan from 2026-11-07) and about **USD 0.24** elsewhere (USD 0.27 on the free plan from 2026-11-07), and up to USD 0.10 more on each if this account read that chart before (USD 0.52 and USD 0.34; on the free plan 0.57 and 0.37). With step 1c's dropship set (the lasting Meta ads, about USD 0.07, USD 0.09 on the free plan, with TikTok's library added in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, about USD 0.06 more; and the AliExpress check on 5, USD 0.063, USD 0.096 on the free plan), add about USD 0.13 elsewhere and USD 0.19 in the EU, the UK and Switzerland: the approval line is then "This costs about USD 0.47 (about USD 0.56 on Apify's free plan), occasionally a little more if I need to dig deeper to answer you" elsewhere (limit USD 0.84), and "This costs about USD 0.71 (about USD 0.82 on Apify's free plan), occasionally a little more if I need to dig deeper to answer you" in the EU, the UK and Switzerland (limit USD 1.23). One yes for all of it, each run's cap its share of the spending limit, never said (`references/running.md`, item 6). Not in the guided first session: there `validate` reads it, as above. With that yes, run the chart and Trends, pick the top 3 for them, and run the ads read on those 3 in the same turn, before the answer (`platforms: ["meta", "tiktok"]` for the EU, the UK and Switzerland). The For you notes then read Felt want from it ("Felt want, from ads: …"). Only if the user declines it is Felt want `unknown`, the ads read being each idea's fix. Never "a long shot" from the leaders' review counts or a seasonal Trends fall: outside the US only thin Search-led demand makes it (the same file, "Outside the US, "a long shot" comes only from thin Search-led demand").
- **The wave read (cold-traffic dropship profiles, after the Where you'd compete read):** offer, as an optional follow-up on the strongest idea for them, whether the advertisers' stores added it in the last 30 days: `cleanpull/shopify-store-scraper` with `stores`: that idea's advertisers' landing domains (marketplaces and link pages set aside, at most 30), `keywords`: its core term, `createdWithinDays: 30`, `waveSummary: true`, `maxProductsPerStore: 1000`, `maxItems: 150`; only matching products are charged, USD 0.0008 each on every plan, so **about USD 0.12 at most** (usually less), with a yes. Pick the stores, confirm the matches against the idea and read it as `references/judgement-saturation.md`, "The wave read" sets out: one line in that idea's For you reading, never counted, never a gate. Not offered in the guided first session.
- **Proven small advertisers (cold-traffic dropship profiles):** offer, as one of the follow-ups, the search for small stores that keep paying for ads on the strongest idea (the `ads-watch` skill's "Proven small advertisers": Meta ads running 35+ days, then the advertisers' stores sized; its estimate, at the yes: about USD 0.10 at most for the first two steps). It shows the idea already works for a store their size, and the angle it uses.

## Step 4, through the cold-traffic lens

- **Through the cold-traffic lens, the For you reading uses the lens's count, not the market's** (`references/judgement-lens-cold-traffic-count.md`, "The count, through this lens"). The market's for/against column stays as it is: it is the market's.
  - **Its reasons:** Felt want (TikTok units) first, then the other rows the lens counts, as it counts them: Search-led demand (a 10,000+ Amazon floor is one caution line, neutral; under 1,000 is against when Felt want is against or `unknown`), Amazon sells it, Where you'd compete, quality gap, and the gates. Amazon chart rank, chart review depth and search trend are context, never a reason for in a For you cell or line.
  - **The opening** names the table's top row: the strongest lead for you is the first row (below), with its TikTok units (outside the US, where TikTok isn't read, its Amazon floor); the market's strongest follows when it differs.
  - **Order** the rows by the For you label (a strong fit, then a fit, with one fix, then a long shot, then not this one), then by the sources backing the idea, counted **only from the rows this lens counts** (`SKILL.md`, step 1c, more first: `references/judgement-ladder.md`, "Each idea's own evidence, and the order of ideas"; TikTok for Felt want, the Amazon floor for Search-led demand and Amazon selling it, the Meta ads that have lasted 35+ days (the proven advertisers this lens reads), AliExpress at 100+ sold for the single-unit gate; never the search trend or the Amazon chart, context here, so a rising trend never sorts an idea ahead of the lens's own count), then by the lens's own count (for minus against over **only the rows this lens counts**: Felt want, Search-led demand, Amazon sells it, Where you'd compete, the quality gap; never the market's for/against column, which includes the search trend and chart reviews this lens doesn't count), then by TikTok units (outside the US, by the Amazon floor). With Where you'd compete and the single-unit check still unread, those counts are usually level, so TikTok units decide: the idea with the most TikTok units among the same label and backing is the top row. The row order is the rank: no "#N for you" in any cell.
  - **Each note says what sets that idea apart** for them, in 8 words or fewer after the label, in the idea's cell, after its label (when it differs from the line above the table) and own fix (`references/judgement-first-answer.md`, "The For you note only tells ideas apart"): a counted row's fact with its number, different on each row (Felt want's units, Amazon selling it, a gate's fact, a note such as bulk); never the search trend or a chart figure, which this lens doesn't count, never a strength as the fix and never a shared check repeated in each note. Notes that would read the same are left out.
  - **What was read, said exactly.** The lasting Meta ads read (step 1c: stores that have kept ads running 35+ days for the niche's keyword) is not the Where you'd compete read (how many stores advertise each idea, active, proven and new). When the first is in and the second isn't, the one line above the table names both as they are ("I read which stores have kept ads running 35+ days for "cat toys"; I haven't yet counted how many stores advertise each idea (the Where you'd compete read)"), never "missing the ads check" or "how many stores run Facebook ads for them" as if no ads were read (`references/judgement-saturation.md`, "Said exactly").
  - **Each label agrees with its own explanation.** Check every note before writing it against gate 3's ordered steps (`references/judgement-lens-cold-traffic.md`, gate 3: first match wins; the user's planned price at or above the break-even is no fail). A note whose explanation is a gate 3 fail under those steps is **not this one**, never "a fit, with one fix". A loss at USD 12 of ads only (profit at USD 8) is a note: never the fix, never a fail.

## Step 4, the For you block

- **For you, through the lens:** only with a profile (`references/judgement-profile.md`, "The seller profile and lenses"). The first sentence names the strongest lead **for you** and its number, then the market's strongest if it differs. Then a short block headed with the lens and the profile as bands in brackets ("For you, through the cold-traffic dropship lens [under USD 1k · dropship · first time · side income]"; never the exact amount, here or anywhere in the answer: a budget line says "your under-USD-1k budget", never the figure they typed): each idea the lens moves up or down or sets aside, with the fact or gate it reacts to (for the cold-traffic lens: TikTok units for felt want; one line saying Amazon search demand (chart reviews, search interest) is a caution, not a lead, for cold traffic; each gate pass / fail / unclear with its fact, unclear when it can't be read from the titles; notes and risk notes as neutral information). The facts in the table are the same for everyone; each idea's For you note, in its cell in the table, carries its reading.
