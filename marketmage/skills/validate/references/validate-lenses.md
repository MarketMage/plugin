# Validate: the lens steps

The parts of the `validate` skill read only with a profile (or a fulfilment or channel the user states), once the lens is chosen, in the same batch as the lens's file: what each lens adds to the runs, how its rows are read, and the For you block. Everything else is in `SKILL.md`.

## Step 1, the Maker lens

- **A maker's own cost, asked once** (the Maker lens: they make what they sell, `references/judgement-lens-maker.md`): in the message with the estimate, one skippable line for their materials and packaging, the time one takes them, and, if they'd like their time counted, their hourly rate (that file, "Their unit cost: materials plus time, asked once"). Never a factory price in its place.

## Step 2, the runs each lens adds

- **Through the Marketplace lens, on Etsy or eBay** (the marketplace they sell on: `references/judgement-lens-marketplace.md`, "Which marketplace is read"): that marketplace's own read is added to round 1, unless this conversation already read it for this keyword with listing rows (`references/running-runs.md`, "Runs in this conversation"): `cleanpull/marketplace-search-scanner` with `keywords: ["<product>"]`, `sources: ["etsy"]` or `["ebay"]`, `maxResults: 24`, `listingRows: true`, as its own run (about USD 0.10; USD 0.19 on Apify's free plan from 2026-11-07, which sets the check's spending limit; its cap is its share of that limit; on Etsy usually 4–5 minutes, sometimes up to about 9: say so with the estimate). Start it **first**, with `waitSecs: 0`, then the rest of round 1; round 1's Amazon scan still runs (it is the market table's source; for them, Amazon's floor is one labelled context line, not their Demand level, which is `unknown`). Answer when the fast reads are in, with that marketplace's For you rows marked provisional ("still reading Etsy, usually 4–5 minutes"), then complete it in a short follow-up when it lands (`references/running-runs.md`, "Timing"). On Amazon, nothing is added: the Amazon rows are theirs.
- **Through the Maker lens** (`references/judgement-lens-maker.md`): they are the supplier, so **round 1 runs no supplier read** (the Made-in-China row is left out, and no AliExpress single-unit check) and there is **no first-order gate**; the margin uses their own making cost. Their marketplace's read (Etsy unless they name another) is added to round 1 exactly as for the Marketplace lens on Etsy or eBay above, with the keyword in the words of their making ("wheel thrown stoneware mug") and `listingRows: true`, and reused when this chat already read it. Amazon's scan and Trends follow **the country they sell from** (named in this chat, else `marketCountry`, else the US: a potter in Vancouver gets amazon.ca and Trends CA), kept for the whole chat; the TikTok Shop read runs **only when they sell from the US**, as context, never counted, and outside the US it is skipped and said once ("TikTok Shop's read covers the US shop only, so it isn't one of your reads"): that file, "Which country the other reads use". The standard check is then about USD 0.22 in the US, with the TikTok context read (about USD 0.31 on Apify's free plan from 2026-11-07), and about USD 0.19 outside the US, with no TikTok read and Amazon's local-market rows (about USD 0.28 on the free plan), with a spending limit of USD 0.47 (USD 0.43 outside the US), from the free-plan figure, never said; usually 4–5 minutes, sometimes up to about 9 for the Etsy read: say so with the estimate. The market table's Margin row uses **their making cost in the supplier cost's place** (`unknown (no supplier read: you make it; needs your making cost)` until they give it). Selling only in their own store or on social pages: Etsy is read as the handmade reference, labelled so (that file). Their price band and concentration come only from the listing rows that match their product, set-asides named with counts (that file, "Their price band: listings that match what they make"); the fairness line goes in their first For you block (that file, "The fairness line").
- **Through the cold-traffic dropship lens, or the Marketplace lens with dropship:** the AliExpress single-unit check is added to round 1, unless this conversation already read it for this idea and ship-to country (`references/running-runs.md`, "Runs in this conversation") (about USD 0.013 more, USD 0.019 on Apify's free plan; it can take several minutes: say so with the estimate, "the AliExpress prices can take several minutes"), since the single-units and profit gates read from it. The standard check is then about USD 0.15 (about USD 0.16 on Apify's free plan), with a spending limit of USD 0.31, never said.

**The single-unit check (dropship).** `cleanpull/aliexpress-scraper` with `searchTerms: ["<product>"]`, `shipTo` (the buyer's country, default `"US"`), `maxResultsPerSearch: 3`, `includeDetails: true` and the `fields` in `references/running-runs.md`, "AliExpress prices" (AliExpress's top 3 results, each sold by the piece, with what a repeat buyer pays and, from the product page, the shipping to the buyer's country), as its own run beside round 1's Made-in-China read (which still gives the market's margin; no `aliexpress` in that read's `sources`). It gives prices, never a worked margin: gate 3 and the margin after ad cost are worked out from them as the lens file says, for the own store (the 2.9% + USD 0.30 card fee unless they gave theirs) and for TikTok Shop (its `marketplaceFees`) alike. Cost: 3 × (USD 0.0007 listing + USD 0.0035 product page) ≈ USD 0.013 (USD 0.019 on Apify's free plan); its cap is its share of the check's spending limit. It can take several minutes (a search page, then 3 product pages), so the answer waits for it once (`waitSecs: 45`) and reads it directly when it is done; when it is still running after that wait, the answer goes without it: what reads from it is then provisional in the first answer and completed in a short follow-up, as for a slow Etsy or eBay read (`references/running-runs.md`, "Timing"). Leave `callOptions.timeout` out.
1. **Start it first in round 1** with `waitSecs: 0` (keep its run ID), then the rest of round 1. Round 2 starts when the rest of round 1 is back, without waiting for this check. **When the other reads are in, wait for it before answering:** `get-actor-run` with `waitSecs: 45`, once (`references/running-runs.md`, "Timing"). **Finished by then (the normal case):** read it as in 2 and 3 below and answer once, with gates 2 and 3 (through the Marketplace lens: gate 2 and the margin row) read from it and the final For you verdict: no provisional mark on them, no follow-up. **Still RUNNING after that wait (the fallback):** say "AliExpress is slow on this one (its product pages can take a few minutes); I'll answer now and add it when it lands", and **answer when the other reads are in**, with gate 2 and gate 3 (through the Marketplace lens: gate 2 and the margin row) named in the For you lines as "AliExpress is slow on this one (its product pages can take a few minutes)" and the For you verdict marked "(provisional)" on them. They read exactly as unread: notes that never cap, and in both lenses "a strong fit" waits for the check to return or miss (the lens file's "Still reading AliExpress"). Then, in the same turn, follow the run as `references/running-runs.md`, "Timing" sets out (the first answer is not the end of the turn: keep following the run in the same turn and write the follow-up when it lands) ("The single-unit check, in that pattern"; a check already running for this product in this conversation is followed, not started again), and when it lands, read it as in 2 and 3 below and send the **short follow-up**: those gates with the actor's numbers, and the final For you verdict through the unchanged gate-3 steps, saying plainly when the label changed ("now: a strong fit", "now: a fit, with one fix") or that it stands.
2. **Read** the `product` rows and the `search-summary`. `price` is what a repeat buyer pays on every order after the first (null: the `priceNote` range, its higher end for gate 3); `regularPrice` is never the cost; a `newShopperPrice` is a one-time new-shopper deal on the first order, said only as that and never used in gate 3 or the margin. **Shipping:** the landed cost is `landedPrice` (`price` + `shippingCost` to `shipTo`) only when `shippingCost` is above 0; a 0, a null or `detailsRead: false` leaves shipping not established for a one-unit order: the cost is "plus shipping, still to add", a margin from it a best case, said so, and never "free shipping" (`references/running-runs.md`, "AliExpress prices: the unit price, shipping, and the new-shopper deal"). Gate 2: a `product` row that is the product passes (AliExpress sells by the piece); it fails **only** when AliExpress answered with product rows and none sells the product as one unit (every row a lot or bulk pack). **No matching listing** (`listingsDelivered: 0` on the search summary): the note "AliExpress showed no matching listing under this name; try another name", never a fail, never a cap; offer one rerun of only the check under a synonym. Gate 3 uses the landed cost, else the cheapest `price` plus shipping still to add, or the user's unit cost when they gave one (the lens file's gates).
3. **AliExpress didn't answer**, in any form (a free `unreadable` row for the idea, a run that failed or was stopped, or no row for the idea at all; nothing is charged for what wasn't read): gate 2 is the note "single units: not confirmed yet (AliExpress didn't answer in time; most consumer goods sell single units there)", and gate 3 is unclear as a note unless the user gave a unit cost; **neither caps the For you verdict** (except where gate 3's ordered steps in `references/judgement-lens-cold-traffic.md` say otherwise: step 2 can fail it and step 1b can make the price the fix, each only under the conditions written there). Offer one rerun of only that check (several minutes) in the next step; never call it a no. When this comes back after the first answer, the follow-up says it in these words. A run that fails outright reads the same way.
4. **The notebook** (if kept) is written with the answer, as usual, and its note updated in the follow-up when the For you label changed.
- **Through the cold-traffic dropship lens: where they'd compete** (round 2, about USD 0.05 more; the standard check is then about USD 0.23, with a spending limit of USD 0.38, never said; reused instead when `find-products` already read it for this idea and country: `references/running-runs.md`, "Runs in this conversation"): `cleanpull/ad-library-monitor` with `keywords: ["<product>"]`, `platforms: ["meta"]`, `countries: ["<market country>"]`, `activeOnly: true`, `oneRowPerAdvertiser: true`, `maxAdsPerTarget: 30` (one page). Cost: 1 watch item USD 0.001 + up to 2 library pages × USD 0.0035 (the actor's cap for 30 ads) + up to 30 advertiser rows × USD 0.0015 ≈ **USD 0.05 at most**. For a market in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, add `"tiktok"` to `platforms` (about USD 0.06 more: 3 pages of 12 ads and up to 30 more rows); TikTok's ads library covers only those markets, so a US read is Meta only, with TikTok Shop (US) as the TikTok side. Its figure goes into the check's one estimate.
- **Through the cold-traffic dropship lens, after the ad library read: the wave read** (optional follow-up, never part of the standard check, never in the guided first session): whether the advertisers' stores added it in the last 30 days. `cleanpull/shopify-store-scraper` with `stores`: the advertisers' landing domains (marketplaces and link pages set aside, at most 30), `keywords`: the idea's core term, `createdWithinDays: 30`, `waveSummary: true`, `maxProductsPerStore: 1000`, `maxItems: 150`; only matching products are charged, USD 0.0008 each on every plan, so **about USD 0.12 at most** (usually less). Offer it in "Build on it" with no price on the offer; on a yes, give its one estimate and run it, or after the ad library read when the user already asked whether stores are adding it now. How to pick the stores, confirm the matches against the idea and read it: `references/judgement-saturation.md`, "The wave read".

## Step 3, the lens rows

- **Their marketplace** (Marketplace lens on Etsy or eBay): its `keyword-summary` row (`source` etsy or ebay): `sellers`, `priceP25`, `priceMedian`, `priceP75` (on eBay, `singleNewPrice` when it left listings out; never on Etsy, which states no condition), `medianReviews` with `reviewScope` (the shop's or the seller's, not the product's), `marketplaceFees`; and the listing rows with `fields: "recordType,source,position,title,price,seller,reviewCount,itemReviews,quantitySold,listedAt,condition,packCount,url"`, each checked against the title as above. → the For you rows of `references/judgement-lens-marketplace.md` (Concentration on their marketplace: the top 3 sellers' share of the listing rows that are the product, with 10 or more such rows, else `unknown`; never the summary's `top3SellerShare` or `sellerHhi`, which cover every organic result; Price band; Margin after its fees; Demand level `unknown`, with Amazon's floor as a labelled context line and `itemReviews` or `quantitySold` as information). Label every figure with its marketplace; never call Amazon's floor Etsy or eBay demand.
- **Where you'd compete** (cold-traffic lens; the `advertiser` rows: `advertiser`, `daysRunning`, `earliestFirstShown`, `adsKept`, `landingDomain`, `sampleAdUrl`): leave out an advertiser whose name and landing domain plainly sell something else (the keyword in an unrelated ad) and say how many; then count the active advertisers, the proven ones (`daysRunning` 35 or more) and the newcomers (14 or fewer), and say "at least" when the read filled its 30 ads. Label the counts with the library and the market country wherever they appear ("14 active advertisers on Meta's US ads"): the coverage line, said once. From the TikTok rows already read: distinct `shopName` among the relevant top 20, and the top 3 shops' share of their units. → the table's *Where you'd compete* row (counts only, not scored for the market), read through the lens (`references/judgement-saturation.md`, "Where you'd compete"). The wave read, when made: its `waveSummary` row and its product rows, each match confirmed against the idea, said as "N of M stores advertising it added it in the last 30 days" with the dates (different products matched by the term said apart; unreadable and not-Shopify stores counted apart; `partial` said); a store wave is not the late wave. One line in the fit check, read with the Where you'd compete reading, never counted, never a gate ("The wave read").

## Step 5, item 8: For you, through the lens

8. **For you, through the lens:** only with a profile (`references/judgement-for-you.md`, "The For you block"): the lens lines (each row that counts differently for them, so through the cold-traffic lens a 10,000+ Amazon floor always gets its one Search-led demand caution line, "those buyers search Amazon and can buy it there"; each gate with the fact read in this run that settles it, "unclear" when that fact wasn't read; notes as information, said once and capping only when three or more point the same way; risk notes in one neutral line), then the **fit check**: first order against the band's upper edge, or, when they named an exact amount in this chat, against that amount privately, said as "fits" or "doesn't fit the amount you gave me" (`references/judgement-lens-own-stock.md`, "Against the amount you gave me") (units × unit or landed cost; AliExpress prices landed when the page charged shipping, else labelled "plus shipping, still to add"), fulfilment against the product (the leaders' weight and size tier from their `fees`, labelled as the leaders', else "weight not read"; never invented freight; for an own-store seller who ships their own orders, the shipping estimate from the weight bands, `references/judgement-rates.md`, "Shipping in the margin", said as an estimate), ad cost against the band (ads in 48, or for cold traffic the margin at USD 8 and USD 12 of ads per sale, stated as an assumption, with the Where you'd compete counts and their reading for this profile, thresholds beside the numbers, then the wave read's line when it was made), and the skills needed. Outside knowledge beyond the lens's gates and risk notes never goes here.
