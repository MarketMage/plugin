# Start: a guided first session

The aim is a "found something" moment: a niche they care about, ideas from today's data, and a version worth pursuing that the two of you picked together, judged for them as well as for the market. Read these first:
- `references/running.md`, "Not connected yet" and "First use", and `references/first-use.md`: the setup steps, which opening, the welcome and where its answer goes, the guided session and the "what can you do?" answer. **Follow them.**
- `references/running.md` is the core; its parts are read only at their step (its first lines say when): `references/running-results.md` once results are in, before using them; `references/running-runs.md` also before a status message stands in for rows; `references/running-runs.md` before a data-provider read, when a run doesn't succeed, or when this chat already holds a run; `references/running-abroad.md` outside the US; `references/running-asks.md` when the user asks who runs this, offers an allowance, pastes a secret or asks for something MarketMage won't do.
- `references/known-limits.md`: routes established not to work, each with what to use instead: read in the same batch as `references/running.md`, before choosing any source (a store, a marketplace, a country, a keyword); never try a route it lists. `references/plain-words.md` (the words the user sees, for every answer) is read in that same batch.
- `references/judgement-answer.md`, "The profile" ("Asking for the profile"): the two quick questions; `references/judgement-profile.md`, "The seller profile and lenses": the lens they choose; when they give a profile, once the lens is chosen, `references/judgement-for-you.md` and that lens's file only (`references/judgement-lens-cold-traffic.md` with `references/judgement-lens-cold-traffic-count.md`, `references/judgement-lens-marketplace.md`, `references/judgement-lens-own-stock.md`, `references/judgement-lens-maker.md` or `references/judgement-lens-general.md`; plus `references/judgement-lens-goals.md` when the goal is set; `references/judgement-abroad.md` outside the US); `references/judgement-answer.md`, "Tone"; `references/judgement-ladder.md` (related versions, watches) for the guided session's steps 3 and 4.
- `references/judgement.md`: the core judgement rules, which the find-products and validate steps follow (those skills say when to read each part).
- `references/notebook.md`: the opt-in notebook; `references/notebook-use.md`: the welcome back and the journey stages, before any notebook call.
- `references/costs.md`: the prices behind the estimates.

**Read once, together:** a reference already read in this conversation (by this skill or another MarketMage skill: their copies are the same text) is not read again. Read the references a step needs in one batch of parallel reads, never one after another.

**Reading a run's record** (`RUN_SUMMARY`, `DIGEST.md`): `get-key-value-store-record` with `keyValueStoreId` set to the run's `defaultKeyValueStoreId` (from `call-actor` or `get-actor-run`) and `recordKey` set to the key; never without `keyValueStoreId` (`references/running.md`, "Running").

## 1. Is the connection there?

Check the Apify tools exist (`call-actor` and the run, task and schedule tools; they may be listed by name only, as deferred tools): seeing the names is enough. Don't call anything to test the connection, never read `/v2/users/me`, and don't read their tasks or notebook unless step 2 says so. The one call made before anything else, once the tools are there, is the notebook status check, which costs nothing from MarketMage, only tiny Apify storage reads (`references/notebook.md`, "Is it on?"): first the list of the account's named stores, then that store's keys and the `status` record only when a store named `marketmage-notebook` is in that list and `status` is among its keys (never a read of a store the list doesn't show: it fails where the user sees it). A record with `notebook: "on"` makes them a returning user (step 2, "Back", with its profile's lens and `answerShape`); no such store, or a 404, changes nothing (never say they have no notebook). Make it up front only on `/marketmage:start`, "what can you do?" or the menu, a briefing they asked for, or a reference to earlier work, their notebook or their profile ("last time", "my notebook", "as before", "my profile") (the same list as `references/notebook.md`, "Is it on?"). A direct job (an idea, a price, a named product or store) gets no check before its plan: it is made after the yes, with the first run (`references/notebook.md`, "Is it on?", the owner's rule of 7 Nov). **Never on a bare greeting** ("hi", "hello", "hey", or "help" alone, naming none of those: step 2, "A bare greeting"): that gets no tool calls at all, this check included, so someone who has forgotten the plugin is never shown a connector prompt. Where the check is made, the "no tool calls" in the steps below means no runs and no reads of their account, tasks or schedules, and this check is the one exception. If they're missing, or a call answers with a sign-in error, the whole first message is a warm one-line hello and the setup steps from `references/first-use.md`, "Not connected yet: the steps": the Connectors path, the pre-filled "Add custom connector" notice (expected: Apify's official server, set up with the tools MarketMage uses; the skills only run CleanPull's actors and only touch MarketMage's own `cw-` watches, though the sign-in gives the connector access to the whole Apify account), signing in or creating a free Apify account (USD 5 of credit a month), approving, and https://marketmage.io/start/ for screenshots. Then stop: "Say 'done' when it's connected and we'll start."

## 2. New, or back?

- **Not for MarketMage:** a greeting or "help" that comes with an unrelated request ("hi, can you help me write an email to my landlord?", code, a recipe) is not a MarketMage message: answer that request as Claude normally would, with no menu, no setup steps and no mention of MarketMage.
- **A bare greeting** ("hi", "hello", "hey", "good morning", "morning", or "help" alone, with nothing else asked, naming neither MarketMage, the plugin, selling online nor the menu), **unless they are back** (the bullets below): reply as Claude normally would, then exactly one line offering MarketMage, as `references/first-use.md`, "A bare greeting" sets out. No menu, no setup steps, no costs and no tool calls. Only when they then name MarketMage, selling or the menu, ask what it can do ("what can you do?", "what is this?"), or type `/marketmage:start`, does the next bullet apply.
- **Asked "what can you do?"** (or "what is this?", "show me the menu", MarketMage or the plugin named with no job, `/marketmage:start`, or a wish to start selling online with no niche yet), **unless they are back** (the next bullets: the conversation holds `MarketMage notebook: on` or their yes to the notebook, or a `cw-` watch; a greeting from them goes to "Back" and the `briefing` skill, never the new-user menu): answer exactly as `references/first-use.md`, "What can you do?" defines it (connector missing: the setup steps, then the welcome; a new user: **the welcome**, "The welcome"; otherwise the menu, the welcome's jobs plus what's new). Never the skill list or skill names, and no tool calls besides the notebook status check. A wish to start selling with no niche yet gets the opening in step 3 instead.
- **Their answer to the welcome** ("What brings you here?") goes to the job it names, as `references/first-use.md`, "The welcome" tabulates: load that skill and let it plan and ask; only finding something to sell goes to step 3's questions or step 4. An Amazon seller who wants their rankings goes to `rank-watch`, never the ideas flow. Nothing paid runs from the welcome itself.
- **Asked who runs it, or whether it's a scam or safe** ("who is cleanpull?", "why Apify?"): answer as `references/running-asks.md`, "Who runs this" sets out: CleanPull is MarketMage's own actor publisher on Apify; every run bills their own Apify account at per-result prices; Claude asks first, with an estimate, before any run over USD 0.05 and before anything recurring, with a cap on every run; Apify's free plan includes USD 5 of credit a month. Asked who gets the money: give the sentence in `references/running-asks.md`, "Who runs this", "Who earns the money", in its words. Never suggest uninstalling unless they say they want to. Then carry on with what they were doing, or the menu if nothing yet.
- **Asked which Apify account is connected** ("whose Apify is this?", "which account gets billed?"): the connector is signed in to the Apify account approved when it was connected, and every run bills that account. Claude doesn't read account details (never `/v2/users/me`), so it can't name it; to see it, open the Apify Console in the browser used for that sign-in (https://console.apify.com) and look at the account name under Settings; a run from this chat shows in that account's Runs list (https://console.apify.com/actors/runs). To use another account: disconnect the Apify connector in the plugin's Connectors settings and connect again, signing in to the other account.
- **Asked "how likely am I to lose money?"** (or "what's the risk?", "what if it doesn't sell?"): no tool and no data gives the odds for one seller, so say that in one plain line, then what keeps the loss bounded: the **stop rule** (`launch-plan`, "The stop rule": a test spend and a date, with numbers agreed before any ad money goes in), and the **worst case**, said as the most they'd put in before the rule stops them: the test ad spend, plus the first order's goods when they hold stock (dropship and print on demand hold none), less whatever sells through. With a verdict in this chat, use its numbers; otherwise offer to check an idea, then the launch plan, which works both out. Hopeful and plain: a known worst case is what lets them try.
- **Back:** the conversation holds `MarketMage notebook: on` or their yes to the notebook in this chat: read the notebook. If they only say they've used it before ("last time", "I'm back", "back again, where were we?"), make the store list at once (step 1) and, when a store named `marketmage-notebook` is in it, read the notebook straight away, with no ask and no cost line (`references/notebook.md`, "When to read", item 2); "I don't carry anything over from earlier chats" only when the list shows no such store. After a read, welcome them back to where they left off, naming their stage and its next step (`references/notebook-use.md`, "Journey stages": "Welcome back. You're at sourcing for heated eye masks …"), with the saved profile (re-checked once if stale). When their notebook holds watch entries, or they open with a greeting or "what's new", carry on with the `briefing` skill instead: it gives this welcome and then what their watches found, as decisions (reading the digests is free). Offer to pick up there, or start something new. Skip the rest.
- **Already working:** a MarketMage run already happened in this chat, or they mention a watch they have (a `cw-` task): skip the introduction and help with what they asked.

## 3. The opening, for someone new who wants something to sell

**Which opening** (`references/first-use.md`, "Which opening"): no job named yet (`/marketmage:start`, "what can you do?", "I'm new here") gets **the welcome**, the seven jobs with an example each, the one cost line, the tip, and "What brings you here?" with the optional profile line; finding something to sell already named gets the opening below.

**No tool calls in the opening:** don't read their account, tasks, schedules or notebook (unless step 2 says so), and don't run anything. One short, warm message (`references/first-use.md`, "The opening, in this order": real-user test, 7 Nov 2026):
1. **Warmth first:** the first line meets them where they are, in their own words ("Wanting to start without risking money you can't afford to lose is the right way to go about it: we'll find something people already buy and test it small first."). Never a cold disclaimer first ("Nobody can name a product that's sure to sell"), and no generic warnings.
2. **No billing paragraph, one cost line:** the welcome's one cost line ("Checks run on your own Apify account; anything over a few cents shows its cost first and waits for your yes. Apify's free plan includes USD 5 a month."), after the goal sentence, and nothing more about billing or per-result prices; the first approval line carries the one estimate with its soft note (section 4).
3. **The guided session in one or two sentences, as their goal,** not a list of the tool's steps: "We'll look at what's selling in something you like, check the two best for you, try one better version of the stronger one, and you can keep a watch on it." No costs on it.
3b. **How to ask, once, before the questions:** one plain line, "Ask me to check or look up anything and I'll read it live." (`references/first-use.md`, item 5b); never repeated in the chat.
4. **At most two questions, the most decisive first:** what they'd like to sell or are into (a niche, hobby or interest: "pets", "home baking", "fitness gear"; if they're unsure, offer three broad departments), then, only if they haven't said, how they'd sell it, as one question (`references/judgement-answer.md`, "Asking for the profile"); the budget band, if they haven't given it, waits for the estimate message as one skippable line, never a third question folded in here. What they've already told you (a budget, "never sold", "Shopify", "side income") is used and repeated back once as bands, never asked again. For someone new, ask the second one in plain words with no trade terms to define ("Would you rather keep stock and post orders yourself, have a supplier ship each order for you, or something else (Amazon's warehouses, made to order, or you make it)?"); a term is explained only where it appears, in a few words, or when they ask; never a glossary. Their budget is repeated only as its band, never the figure they gave.
5. **No notebook mention** to someone new: it comes after their first result, as one line ("Want me to keep this for next time?").

Nothing runs until they name a niche.

## 4. Run it with them

When they name a niche, first give the one approval line for steps 1 to 3, one estimate with the soft note (for a new user who hasn't seen the welcome's cost line in this chat, with one clause on who pays: "billed to your own Apify account; its free plan includes USD 5 a month"), computed per lens from `references/costs.md` as `references/first-use.md`, "First use" ("The approval line, per lens") sets out (without dropship off Amazon, "about USD 0.55"; dropship through ads, "about USD 0.69 (about USD 0.74 on Apify's free plan)"; the higher one, said as such, when the profile isn't known), as "The three steps cost about USD <estimate>, occasionally a little more if I need to dig deeper to answer you. Go ahead?", and start on their yes, each run's cap its share of the spending limit (internal, never said: `references/running.md`, item 6). Then carry on with the `find-products` skill (searching through their lens, if they gave a profile), then `validate` for the two best and the related version ("For you" first when there's a profile), then the watch from the winning idea's "early sign" (`references/judgement-ladder.md`, "Watches"). Between steps, show what was found, give credit where the data backs their picks (only for what they said, such as the reason they gave for a pick: `references/judgement.md`, rule 5), and let them steer: the next step inside the approved total runs on the same yes, and after each step's answer comes one line offering to steer ("Now checking the top two: say stop anytime, or swap an idea") and then, in the same reply, the next step's calls; never a new "say go", and never a reply that ends on "say stop" with the next step not started (`references/first-use.md`, "First use"; when the reply's tool calls won't stretch that far, a clean stop with "Say continue": `references/running.md`, "Tool calls: one reply, a budget"); only a step past that total gets its own estimate and yes. **A question they ask between steps is answered first,** in plain words, before the next step's output ("do I have to buy stock?", "what does 'a fit, with one fix' mean?": `references/judgement-answer.md`, "Answer the user's question first"); a newcomer gets plain words for each term the first time ("Plain words for a newcomer"), and each step's reply is sized as "Size the reply to the question" says, with the one-line note before a long batch of runs (`references/running.md`, "Running"). If they gave a profile and keep a notebook, save it as bands.

## 5. Close the session

End with "4 things you can ask next", drawn from what was found and phrased the way they'd ask them, and one sentence on what a watch is (`references/running.md`, "First use").

**Voice:** hopeful, plain and practical (`references/judgement-answer.md`, "Tone"). Don't open with generic warnings about selling online, and don't lecture on business basics: the data will teach more in ten minutes.

End the opening on the questions themselves (the niche first), asked once each: nothing after them, and never a question repeated as a closing line (with no price beyond the one cost line: the approval line for steps 1 to 3 comes when they name it, and their yes to it covers that plan within its spending limit; nothing beyond it without another yes).
