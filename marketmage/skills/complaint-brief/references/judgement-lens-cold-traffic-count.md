# Judgement: the cold-traffic dropship lens, the count

The second file of the cold-traffic dropship lens (`judgement-lens-cold-traffic.md`, with the platform rules, the rows, the margin after ad cost and the gates). Read in the same batch as it.

**Unclear gates in this lens:** an unanswered or unread gate 2 or 3 is a note that never caps (as above) and counts as not failed: it never pushes an idea below "a fit, with one fix" (gate 3's steps 1b and 2 are the exceptions, under their conditions). The one thing an unread gate 3 holds back is "a strong fit", which needs it passed (step 1 with a read unit cost, or step 5: the count, below). Only an unclear gate 1 (a title close to a rule whose wording doesn't settle it) caps at "a fit, with one fix", the fix being the platform's review of that rule. Gate 3's pass **with the price as the fix** also caps there (it is a pass, not an unclear gate).

**Still reading AliExpress.** The single-unit check usually returns within its wait (under a minute) and is then read like any read that is in, with none of what follows; only when it is still running after that wait (AliExpress is slow on this one: 5 to 20 minutes) does the first answer go without it (`running-runs.md`, "Timing"). Until it lands, gates 2 and 3 are read exactly as unread: gate 2 is the still-reading note above, and gate 3 goes through its ordered steps without a read unit cost (the user's unit cost, when they gave one, counts as usual), its step 3 said as "profit: AliExpress is slow on this one (it can take 5 to 20 minutes)". So each is a note that never caps, and steps 1b and 2 still apply under their own conditions. While the check is still running, and only then, the verdict can't be "a strong fit", even when gate 3 passes on the user's own unit cost (gate 2 is still unread and could fail): a count that reads strong is "a fit, with one fix (provisional)", the fix being the AliExpress price, already being read (no cost to offer). Once the check has returned or missed, the count below applies unchanged. The For you verdict is marked "(provisional)" on those gates. When the run lands, the short follow-up reads gates 2 and 3 from it through the same ordered steps, with the actor's numbers (the MOQ-1 price before shipping, the break-even, the margin at USD 8 and 12 of ads), and gives the final For you verdict, saying plainly when the label changed ("now: a strong fit", "now: a fit, with one fix") or that it stands.

**The count, through this lens** (first match wins):
- **not this one:** a gate fails (1, 2 or 3), naming the fact read in this run and the related version that would pass it. Nothing else makes it;
- **a long shot:** no gate fails, but demand on the channel they'd sell through is thin: **Felt want is against** (the top TikTok Shop listing under 100 units, for example 94 units ever across 11 shops), or, where TikTok Shop isn't read (outside the US), Search-led demand is against for thinness (an Amazon floor under 1,000 units a month). **Thin demand stays thin:** say plainly that the feed shows little demand, with its numbers (and the Amazon floor beside it), and never call low Amazon demand an opening. Hopeful and honest: it can still work, so the answer says **what would have to be true** (in one line, from the data: "buyers who haven't found it on TikTok yet would have to stop for it in a feed") and **the cheapest test** that would show it (the best ladder rung's check, for example `validate` on a version buyers already want, or a small ad test the user runs themselves, priced from their band). **When search demand is real but the feed's is thin** (Felt want against and an Amazon floor of 1,000+ units a month), the first related version is **the channel switch**, with both numbers: "buyers search for it (Amazon floor 6,550 a month) but don't impulse-buy it on TikTok (best listing 51 units): sell it where people search", meaning search ads (Google Shopping) to their own store, or a marketplace listing (Amazon, eBay, Walmart, or Etsy for a handmade or personalised version), each with what changes (search ads are paid per click rather than per view; Amazon needs stock or a seller-of-record setup with a supplier who ships under their name). This switch comes before the cheapest-test line, and the ladder's channel rung (`judgement-ladder.md`) is this one;
- **a strong fit:** Felt want favours, no counted row is against, no gate fails and gate 1 is not unclear, gate 3 **passed** (its step 1 with a read unit cost, or step 5; not unclear, not with the price as the fix), **Where you'd compete was read** (not `unknown`), and no three notes point against. "Strong" is a claim the data has to back, so an unclear gate 3 or an unread Where you'd compete never reaches it: the idea is **a fit, with one fix**, the fix being the missing read, named from the skill running it (the single-unit check, the read that shows a price buyers pay, or the ad library read), never lower for being unread. Gate 2 unanswered (AliExpress didn't answer) doesn't block it on its own;
- **a fit, with one fix:** everything else. The fix is the one that matters most: an unclear policy gate's rule (gate 1), the price from gate 3 when it passed with the price as the fix, the read that would confirm a strong fit (what gate 3 is missing, Where you'd compete) when that is all that stands between it and strong, the TikTok read when Felt want is `unknown`, an against row (Amazon sells it; Where you'd compete, whose fix is an unserved angle), or the three notes that cap it.

A provisional verdict while a slow read is still running (`running-runs.md`, "Never hold the whole answer for a slow read") uses the same labels, marked "(provisional)" after the label, never a fifth label.

Felt want is this lens's deciding row for a strong fit, so with a cold-traffic profile in the US the TikTok read is part of the check: never left unread as "settled early" because the market verdict is settled.

**Felt want outside the US.** TikTok Shop is read only in the US (`running.md`, "Country"), so for a seller in another country Felt want is read from a stand-in instead of staying `unknown`, and the row names it as one. In this order:
1. **The feed's ads in their own country:** the Where you'd compete read (part of the check through this lens), with TikTok's ads library added for the EU, the UK and Switzerland. At the Several proven threshold (**3 or more proven advertisers**, running 35 days or more; `judgement-saturation.md`, rules of thumb, said as such) in one library's read, Felt want favours ("Felt want, from ads: 4 advertisers have kept Meta ads running 35+ days in the UK; ads that last are selling"); active advertisers with fewer proven is neutral; no active advertiser in any library read is `unknown` (no ads is not thin demand). The same read feeds Where you'd compete: say so once, so the two rows aren't read as two separate facts.
2. **The US TikTok Shop feed, as a labelled proxy**, when the ads read leaves Felt want `unknown` or the user asks for it: the TikTok read in the US, scored on the row's thresholds and labelled "US TikTok Shop, a proxy: your country's feed may differ". A proxy that favours or reads neutral counts like the real row; one that reads against is a line, neutral in the count, and never makes "a long shot" (outside the US that still comes from Search-led demand, as the count says).

With a stand-in that favours, "a strong fit" is reachable outside the US on the same terms as in the US. **The one fix is never a read that can't run for them:** outside the US, when Felt want is `unknown`, the fix (or the check named after "a fit, with one fix") is the stand-in read that settles it (the ads read with TikTok's library for an EU, UK or Swiss market, or the US TikTok proxy), never "the TikTok read" or TikTok Shop data for their country.

**The stand-in runs with the check, not after it.** Outside the US, the ads read in step 1 is how this lens reads Felt want at all, so it is in the first estimate and runs with the first yes, on the top ideas as soon as the chart and Trends name them, in the same turn and before the answer (the skill running the check says how). It is never left as an optional add-on to ask about later; only the user declining it leaves Felt want `unknown`, and then the fix is that read.

**Proven advertisers checked on TikTok, beside the stand-in (EU, UK, Switzerland).** TikTok's library can't be searched for long-running ads by keyword (a keyword search returns the newest ads first, with no date filter, and gives no account ids to size from), so the TikTok side of the stand-in is read by name: the proven advertisers the Meta read found, or brands the user names, checked on TikTok with `cleanpull/ad-library-monitor`, `advertisers: [<their names>]` (at most 10), `platforms: ["tiktok"]`, `activeOnly: true`, `oneRowPerAdvertiser: true`, `maxAdsPerTarget: 12` (no `tiktokAccountSize`: the named advertiser's own read fills the account size when TikTok gives it, free). Each advertiser row then carries `daysRunning` (at least: the oldest of the newest page), `maxReach` (the widest reach band among its kept ads) and, when present, `accountAdsInLibrary` (how many ads TikTok's library lists for the account). It costs up to about **USD 0.033 a name** (1 check, up to 4 library requests at USD 0.0035, up to 12 rows at USD 0.0015; about USD 0.006 when a name matches one account; about USD 0.047 a name at most on the Free plan from 2026-11-07): offered after the Meta read with that cost, its own part of the yes, never folded into the stand-in's figure. When someone asks for TikTok's proven advertisers on a product, say plainly the library can't be searched that way and offer this check by name; the labelled US TikTok Shop proxy (step 2 above) stays as it is. Reading it:
- **Proven** is an ad running 35 days or more; **small** is about **50 or fewer ads** in TikTok's library, an order of magnitude (TikTok's count varies between reads), each said as a rule of thumb; `accountAdsInLibrary` missing or null is "size not read", never small. Sorted by `maxReach`, widest first. There is no store step: TikTok rows have no landing page.
- **It counts the same:** advertisers proven on TikTok count toward the Several proven threshold as one library's read, exactly as in step 1 above; the sizes add one line of facts, never a different count: "2 of the Meta advertisers also keep TikTok ads running 35+ days with a few dozen ads or fewer: small sellers are making TikTok ads pay here." Big advertisers are information, never against.

**Outside the US, "a long shot" comes only from thin Search-led demand** (an Amazon floor under 1,000 units a month, with Felt want's stand-in against or `unknown`: a stand-in that favours or reads neutral rules it out). Large review counts on the leaders, a seasonal fall in Trends or a high price are never thin demand: they are the market's rows (review depth, the trend), read in the count as they are, or the one fix ("you need a better product than the leader": an angle), never "a long shot" on their own.

**Worked examples** (cold-traffic dropship, under $1k unless said):
- Heated eye mask at USD 29.99, Strong for the market: TikTok top 5 at 1,200 to 3,400 units (Felt want favours); a 14,000-unit Amazon floor (one caution line, neutral beside that felt want); no Amazon-sold leaders; the single-unit check's MOQ-1 offer at USD 6.20 before shipping (`moq1OfferFound: true`, `unitPriceMin` 6.20: gate 2 passes); break-even at USD 8 of ads well under the ceiling (TikTok listings at USD 29.99 with 1,200+ units) and under the USD 29.99 price (gate 3 passes, shipping still to add); "rechargeable" and "for dry eyes" in the titles (risk notes); Where you'd compete: 14 active Meta advertisers in 30 ads (crowded at 10+), 5 running 35+ days, 3 new in the last 14 days (no late wave), the top 3 TikTok shops 42% of units. → Where you'd compete is **against** for a first-timer under $1k (you'd be outbid while testing), so **For you: a fit, with one fix**: an angle no current advertiser targets, such as the Bluetooth sleep-sound version (+400% in rising searches), checked against their ads. Not "not this one": every gate passes.
- The same heated eye mask, the same counts, for a seller with $5k–25k who has sold with a brand (the outlasting reading): Where you'd compete **favours** (crowded, with 5 proven advertisers of 14, 3+ needed: demand that pays). → **For you: a strong fit**; the fit check says the landed cost is still to read. At $1k–5k with no experience given (the middle reading), the same counts read neutral, with the unserved angle as a line.
- Clip-on phone light at USD 14.99, Not this version for the market: top TikTok listing 80 units (Felt want against), Amazon floor 600 (against: thin stays thin), MOQ-1 at USD 1.40 before shipping, break-even at USD 8 of ads (1.40 + 8 + 0.30) ÷ 0.971 = 9.99, under the USD 14.99 a top-10 Amazon leader sells at (300+ a month), so gate 3 passes. → **For you: a long shot**: demand is thin in both sources (80 TikTok units, a 600-unit Amazon floor), though no gate fails. What would have to be true: feed buyers would have to stop for it although only 80 units have sold on TikTok Shop; the cheapest test: `validate` on a version buyers already want (the ladder's best rung), about USD 0.23. Not "not this one": no gate fails.
- Desk converter at USD 89, Promising for the market: 94 units ever across 11 TikTok Shop shops (Felt want against), every gate passes. → **For you: a long shot**, not "a fit, with one fix": thin demand on their own channel outranks any one fix.
- Cable organiser at USD 6.99 with an MOQ-1 offer at USD 1.10: 6.99 − 1.10 − the fee − 8 is negative at the low ad cost; break-even is USD 9.69 (at 2.9% + 0.30), but the highest price with sales behind it is USD 7.99 (a top-10 leader selling 400+ a month), with no TikTok listing above it. → **For you: not this one**: gate 3 fails on those numbers; the related version is a bundle priced where USD 8 of ads leaves profit.
- Magnetic lashes at Amazon's median of USD 12.99 with an MOQ-1 offer at USD 6.93: 12.99 − 6.93 − 0.68 payment fee − 8 = −2.62 at the low ad cost; break-even USD 15.69, quoted as USD 15.99 ((6.93 + 8 + 0.30) ÷ 0.971 at a 2.9% + 0.30 payment fee), and a TikTok listing at USD 19.99 has 870 units sold (the ceiling). → Gate 3 **passes with the price as the fix**: **For you: a fit, with one fix**, "price it from USD 15.99 in your store (buyers already pay USD 19.99 on TikTok)". Not "not this one": a price buyers pay leaves profit.
- Walking-pad mat: best TikTok listing 51 units (Felt want against), Amazon floor 6,550 a month, every gate passes. → **For you: a long shot** on TikTok ads, and the first related version is the channel switch: buyers search for it, so search ads to their store or a marketplace listing, with what changes.
- The same heated eye mask, first-timer under $1k, when AliExpress didn't answer the single-unit check (`moq1OfferFound: null`, `aliexpress: "no answer"`): gate 2 is the note "single units: not confirmed yet (AliExpress didn't answer in time; most consumer goods sell single units there)" and gate 3 is unclear as a note (no unit cost read); neither caps, so the label comes from the other rows (**a fit, with one fix**, the fix still the unserved angle), with one rerun of the check offered. Had AliExpress answered with 3 offers and none sold singly (`offersRead: 3`, `moq1OfferFound: false`) → **not this one**: gate 2 fails on that fact. Had it answered with no matching listing (`offersRead: 0`) → the note "AliExpress showed no matching listing under this name; try another name", no cap, with one rerun under a synonym offered.
- The same heated eye mask while the single-unit check is still running: gate 2 "single units: AliExpress is slow on this one (it can take 5 to 20 minutes)" and gate 3 "profit: AliExpress is slow on this one (it can take 5 to 20 minutes)", notes that never cap. For the first-timer under $1k → **For you: a fit, with one fix (provisional)**, the fix still the unserved angle; the follow-up, once the MOQ-1 offer at USD 6.20 lands: gates 2 and 3 pass, the label stands. For the $5k–25k seller who has sold with a brand → **a fit, with one fix (provisional)**, the fix the AliExpress price, already being read; the follow-up: gates 2 and 3 pass, "now: a strong fit". The same while running if they had given their own unit cost of USD 6 (gate 3 passes on it): still **a fit, with one fix (provisional)**, since gate 2 is unread.
- Glass terrarium kit, fragile, little visible motion: two notes (fragile, not demo-able) → said, no cap. A third note resting on a fact (the margin negative at USD 12) → at most a fit, with one fix.

Channels: TikTok Shop and their own store come first in the ladder's channel rung, except when search demand is real but the feed's is thin (the long-shot channel switch above): then search ads to their store, or a marketplace listing, come first.
