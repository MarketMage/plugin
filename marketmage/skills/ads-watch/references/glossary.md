# Glossary

Plain meanings for trade terms. Use these words the first time a term appears in an answer, briefly ("MOQ, the minimum order"), and the same way every time.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ASIN | Amazon's ID for a product listing (B0…), one per marketplace. |
| Best Sellers Rank (BSR) | A product's sales rank in an Amazon category, updated hourly. Lower sells more. It's relative to the category: 5,000 in Kitchen sells far more than 5,000 in Industrial. Amazon publishes no sales figures, so this is a proxy. |
| Bought in past month | Amazon's own rounded badge ("6K+ bought in past month"). The lower bound is the closest public figure to monthly units. |
| Buy Box | The "Add to cart" seller on an Amazon listing when several sellers offer it. Losing it means most sales go to another seller. |
| MAP | Minimum advertised price: the lowest price a brand allows resellers to show. Below it is a violation of the brand's policy, not of the law. |
| GTIN, UPC, EAN | Barcode numbers that identify a product across stores (UPC in North America, EAN elsewhere; GTIN covers both). |
| MOQ | Minimum order quantity: the fewest units a supplier will sell in one order. |
| Unit price, price break | What one unit costs; a price break is a lower unit price at a larger quantity. |
| Landed cost | Unit price plus shipping spread over the units. Here it excludes duties, taxes, payment fees and customs brokerage unless stated. |
| Margin (before fees) | (selling price − unit or landed cost) ÷ selling price. Marketplace fees, ads and returns come out of it. |
| p25, median, p75 | The prices a quarter, half and three quarters of the way up the list: half the offers sit between p25 and p75. |
| Top-3 share | The share of the top results held by the three biggest sellers or brands. |
| HHI | Herfindahl–Hirschman index: a 0 to 1 measure of how concentrated sellers are; above 0.25 a few sellers dominate. |
| Organic and sponsored | Organic results are earned by the listing; sponsored ones are paid ads. Ads rotate on every visit. |
| Compare-at price | The crossed-out "was" price a store shows next to a sale price. A store that shows one all year is using it as a pricing model, not a sale. |
| Free-shipping threshold | The basket value above which a store ships free. |
| Google Trends interest | Search interest on a 0 to 100 scale relative to the peak in the period and place chosen: a shape, not a search volume. |
| Units per day (TikTok Shop) | Units sold between two reads of the same product, divided by the days between them. One read gives lifetime units only. |
| Watch, watchlist | A saved Apify task with a schedule that reads the same items each time and reports what changed. |
| Baseline | A watch's first read, which everything after is compared with. It reads everything, so it costs more than a typical run. |
| Spending limit (spending cap, max cost per run) | The most a run is allowed to cost: internal, and never said for a one-off read (`maxTotalChargeUsd` for a run started in chat; the approval gives one estimate with the soft note); a watch's `maxRunCostUsd` is the exception, said as its "spending limit" ("Max cost per run (USD)" in the Console). |
| Sales floor | The sum of Amazon's "bought in past month" badges over the top 10, each at the bottom of its bucket ("1K+" counts 1,000), and that times their prices: at least what they sell a month, never an estimate. |
| Referral fee, FBA fee | Amazon's cut of each sale (a share of the price, 15% in most categories) and its charge for storing, packing and shipping a unit when Amazon fulfils it (set by size and weight). |
| Margin after fees | What a sale pays out after the marketplace's fees, less the unit cost, as a share of the price; freight, duty and ads still to come. |
